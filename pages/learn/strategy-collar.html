⚠️ Common Misconceptions
  • Misconception 1: "A Zero-Cost Collar is completely free."
    Reality: It costs zero cash upfront, but its opportunity cost is relinquishing all upside above KCK_CKC​. Furthermore, because of Negative Equity Volatility Skew (OTM puts have much higher implied volatility than OTM calls), to get a 10%10\%10% OTM put for "free," you often have to cap your upside at only +6%+6\%+6% or +7%+7\%+7%! Wait to put on collars when skew is relatively flat, or accept a small net debit to keep a wider upside cap.
  • Misconception 2: "Putting a tight collar around a low-basis stock is always tax-free."
    Reality: Under IRC Section 1259 (Constructive Sales Rules), if an executive puts on a collar that is too tight (eliminating substantially all risk of loss and opportunity for gain), the IRS treats the collar as an immediate Constructive Sale on Day 1, triggering immediate capital gains tax on the entire unrealized appreciation! Tax counsel generally requires maintaining at least a 15%–20%15\%–20\%15%–20% band width between KPK_PKP​ and KCK_CKC​ to avoid constructive sale treatment.
