Reformulated Balance Sheet

Primary: Stephen H. Penman Ch. 8 · also: Peter D. Easton Ch. Mod. 3
Intuition

Standard GAAP/IFRS balance sheets group assets and liabilities by liquidity (current vs. non-current). Stephen Penman's reformulated balance sheet regroups them by business purpose: operating activities vs. financing activities. Operating activities create value through customer products and services; financing activities merely raise the capital to fund operations. Untangling the two isolates Net Operating Assets (NOA) and Net Financial Obligations (NFO).

📝 Notes for Future-Me

Operating cash flow is where alpha lives. If a business appears to generate higher ROE simply because it parked cash in 5% Treasury bills or took on excessive cheap debt, standard DuPont will disguise this. Reformulating the balance sheet strips out treasury noise and exposes true core operating returns.
