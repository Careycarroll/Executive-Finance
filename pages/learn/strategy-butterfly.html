⚠️ Common Misconceptions
  • Misconception 1: "A 4:14:14:1 reward-to-risk ratio means the Butterfly starts doubling in value two weeks before expiration."
    Reality: Because the Long Options at K1K_1K1​ and K3K_3K3​ decay at almost the same rate as the Short Options at K2K_2K2​ early in the trade, a Butterfly's mark-to-market price barely moves until the final 5 to 10 days before expiration! Do not buy 60-day Butterflies expecting rapid Theta profits; Butterflies are inherently short-dated pin-targeting vehicles.
  • Misconception 2: "Call Butterflies and Put Butterflies require the same cash outlay."
    Reality: When targeting a price below current spot (K2<S0K_2 < S_0K2​<S0​), using Out-of-the-Money Puts (a Put Butterfly) avoids dealing with wide bid-ask spreads and early-exercise assignment risk on Deep In-the-Money Calls. Always build your Butterfly using Out-of-the-Money options (Put Fly for downside targets; Call Fly for upside targets; Iron Fly for ATM targets).
