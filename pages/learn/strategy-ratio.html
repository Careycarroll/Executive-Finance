⚠️ Common Misconceptions
  • Misconception 1: "A Put Ratio Spread entered for a net credit is a low-risk trade because you can't lose if the stock goes up."
    Reality: While you keep the $1.00\$1.00$1.00 credit if the stock rallies, below the lower breakeven (2K2−K1−Credit2 K_2 - K_1 - \text{Credit}2K2​−K1​−Credit) you are naked short a put! During a market crash or overnight bankruptcy gap, a 1×21 \times 21×2 Put Ratio Spread suffers catastrophic losses combined with exploding implied volatility (short Vega). Only enter a Put Ratio Spread if you are 100% willing and capitalized to take physical ownership of the stock at the effective breakeven price (Keff=$84K_{\text{eff}} = \$84Keff​=$84).
  • Misconception 2: "Backspreads are better than buying naked calls or puts."
    Reality: In a 1×21 \times 21×2 Call Backspread (Short 1 100C100\text{C}100C / Long 2 105C105\text{C}105C), there is a "Valley of Death" right at ST=$105S_T = \$105ST​=$105 where you suffer maximum loss ($5.00\$5.00$5.00). If the stock rallies gently and pins at $105\$105$105 on expiration day, your directional call was right—yet you lose the maximum possible amount on the trade!
📝 Notes for Future-Me

How institutional equity managers use the Stock Repair Strategy (a 1×21 \times 21×2 Call Ratio Spread): Suppose you bought 100 shares of a high-quality stock at $100\$100$100, and it dropped to $80\$80$80 (−$20-\$20−$20 unrealized loss). Instead of waiting years for a +25%+25\%+25% rally back to $100\$100$100 or doubling down with new cash:

  1. Keep your 100 shares at $80\$80$80.
  2. Buy 1 ATM $80\$80$80 Call and Sell 2 OTM $90\$90$90 Calls for $0.00\$0.00$0.00 net cost (a 1×21 \times 21×2 Call Ratio Spread).
  3. Because you already own 100 shares of stock, the extra short $90\$90$90 Call is 100% covered (zero naked upside risk!).
  4. Now, if the stock merely recovers halfway to $90\$90$90, you earn $10\$10$10 on your stock plus $10\$10$10 on your $80/$90\$80/\$90$80/$90 call spread—breaking even completely at $90\$90$90 instead of $100\$100$100 for zero out-of-pocket cash!
