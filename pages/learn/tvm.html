Time Value of Money

Primary: Gabriel Hawawini Ch. 2 · also: Zvi Bodie Ch. 5
Intuition

A dollar received today is worth more than a dollar received tomorrow because a dollar today can be invested to earn a positive return, and because inflation erodes future purchasing power. Every valuation model across corporate finance and investments is built on this foundation: converting cash flows across disparate points in time into a common denominator today.

  • PVPVPV: Present Value — the current worth of a future stream of cash flows discounted at rate rrr.
  • CFtCF_tCFt​: Cash Flow at time ttt — the nominal dollar amount expected at period ttt.
  • rrr: Discount Rate / Opportunity Cost of Capital — the expected rate of return for an investment of equivalent risk.
  • ttt: Period index (1,2,…,T1, 2, \dots, T1,2,…,T).
  • TTT: Terminal period of the horizon.
📝 Notes for Future-Me

Always remember: discounting is division by (1+r)t(1+r)^t(1+r)t, compounding is multiplication by (1+r)t(1+r)^t(1+r)t. If you ever find yourself struggling to build intuition on complex structured products or exotic debt schedules, collapse the problem back into discrete cash flow streams and discount them period by period.
