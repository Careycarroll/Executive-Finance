⚠️ Common Misconceptions
  • Misconception 1: "Because an Iron Condor consists of two vertical spreads with 5widtheach,mybrokerrequires5 width each, my broker requires5widtheach,mybrokerrequires10 of margin."
    Reality: The underlying stock cannot be below $85\$85$85 and above $115\$115$115 at the exact same time on expiration day! Because only one side can ever lose at expiration, options exchanges only require margin for one single wing width (W=$5.00W = \$5.00W=$5.00), minus the $2.00\$2.00$2.00 credit collected, resulting in a maximum capital requirement of just $3.00\$3.00$3.00!
  • Misconception 2: "You should hold an Iron Condor all the way to expiration day to collect 100% of the credit."
    Reality: Holding a short Iron Condor into the final week before expiration is a notorious retail mistake. In the final 7 days, Gamma explodes: a 1.5%1.5\%1.5% afternoon move in the stock can swing the Condor from +80%+80\%+80% profit to −100%-100\%−100% max loss, and Pin Risk between K1K_1K1​ and K2K_2K2​ can leave you assigned on 100 shares of stock over the weekend with your protective wing expired! Professional traders systematically take profits at 50%50\%50% of max credit or exit at 14–21 days to expiration.
